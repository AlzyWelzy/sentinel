Scope: the whole web app (landing, auth, app shell, every app screen, public status pages). Mode: Operate for the app; the landing page (/) is Persuade inside the same world.
Audience: small hosting/MSP teams and solo devs equally (PRODUCT.md). Job: see what failed, act, confirm recovery.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The fleet is a labelled distribution panel. Every server is a numbered circuit, every failure a tripped breaker with its reason typed on the directory card. Refuses the category default of floating KPI cards, glow and gradients.

OWN-WORLD: Powder-coated enamel ground (cool grey in light, charcoal in dark), engraved phenolic nameplates (ink plate, light engraved condensed caps) as headings and primary buttons, a typed directory card (ruled rows, mono figures) for tables. Severity on one fixed ramp: energized green, amber, trip orange, idle grey; plate blue only for links and focus. Square 3px corners, 1px rules as the only containers, no gradients, no glass, no glow.

STORY: An operator opens Sentinel and sees the panel: what is energized, what tripped and why, in one glance; they open the tripped circuit, act, and watch it reset.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Overview: a nameplate title, a one-line feeder strip (servers / tripped / checks / incidents counts as engraved readouts), then the panel: numbered circuits in two ruled columns, tripped circuits first in orange with their typed reason. Primary action (Add server) is an ink nameplate button top right. Landing: the panel itself as the hero, beside the claim and Sign up.

FORM: Panel Schedule, position 6 of the ordered grounded list; seed key 23eed811. Raises: fixed severity ramp (star atlas); whole-cell dense fleet packing (event catalog); no card chrome, rules are containers (metro tiles); topology lines 90/45 only, failing feeder brightest (transit); shared sparkline scale (botanical). Signature interaction: a circuit trips/resets with a breaker-handle flip (transform 160ms, strong ease-out), nothing else animates on repeat views.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
